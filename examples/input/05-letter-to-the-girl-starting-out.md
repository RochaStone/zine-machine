# A letter to the girl starting out

You do not need a website yet. You do not need a logo, a launch plan or a business card with foil on it.

You need ten people who love what you make, and the nerve to ask them to tell ten more.

Make the thing. Show the thing. Charge for the thing. Then make it again, a little braver.

> Everything I want wants me more.

See you at the wheel.
