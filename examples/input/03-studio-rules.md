# Studio rules (taped above the wheel)

- Glaze is a mood, not a mistake.
- Music loud, notifications off.
- Every wonky cup has a home.
- Snacks are a business expense.
- If it cracks, it was practice.

I wrote these on a receipt the week everything went wrong. Three kiln loads cracked, a wholesale order fell through and I cried into a bucket of slip. The rules stayed up. So did I.
