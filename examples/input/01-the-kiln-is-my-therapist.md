# The kiln is my therapist

Every Sunday I load the kiln and leave my phone in the car. Eleven hours of heat. Nothing I can rush, nothing I can fix by refreshing an inbox.

I used to think slow was a luxury. Now I think slow is the business plan. The mugs that sell out are the ones I made when I was not trying to sell anything.

> Slow is not the opposite of ambitious. Slow is how ambitious people stay in love with the work.

So this is your sign. Pick one thing this week you refuse to rush.
