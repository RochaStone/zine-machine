# Price it like you mean it

My first bowls were twelve dollars. I was so scared nobody would buy them that I priced them like an apology.

Here is what changed when I tripled the price:

- the right people started buying
- they sent photos of the bowls on their tables
- I stopped resenting the hours

Your price is the first story people hear about your work. Make it a good one. Mine now says: this was made slowly, by hand, by someone who knows exactly what it is worth.
