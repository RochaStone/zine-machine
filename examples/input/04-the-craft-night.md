# What happened at craft night

Twelve strangers, one long table, a lot of clay under fingernails. I thought I was teaching them to pinch a pot. They thought they were coming for a hobby.

By ten o'clock two of them had swapped numbers, one had quit her job in her head, and everyone had made something lopsided and perfect.

**Nobody posted a single story.** They were too busy being there.

That is the whole point of the room. Come to the next one. Bring a friend who needs it.
